# Dropreel AI Video

Build a professional one-page marketing/landing site for "Dropreel", a service that produces AI-generated Video Sales Letters (VSLs) and ad creatives for ecommerce and dropshipping brands (the kind of short vertical/square video ads used on Meta and TikTok).

TARGET AUDIENCE: ecommerce and dropshipping store owners who need a steady stream of ad video creatives (often at volume, e.g. multiple videos per week) and are currently either paying too much for traditional production or getting robotic-looking results from generic AI tools.

LANGUAGE: Bilingual site with an EN/ES language switcher in the header (default language: English). All copy (hero, sections, FAQ, form labels, buttons) must exist in both languages via the switcher, not just auto-translated placeholders — write real copy for both.

POSITIONING — IMPORTANT: Do NOT present this as a fixed menu of "3 products" or named service tiers. The offering is open: "we produce any type of AI-made VSL for your ecommerce brand" — talking AI avatars, voiceover-narrated videos over real footage/b-roll, fully animated 3D characters, or anything else the brand's product needs. The portfolio/examples section should show varied video examples side by side as proof of range, without labeling them as separate purchasable products or tiers.

NO PRICING ANYWHERE ON THE SITE. Every CTA leads to a "get a quote" contact step — never show numbers or price ranges.

STRUCTURE (mirror the polish/flow of a premium SaaS landing page like Reveniq, adapted to this offer):

1. Hero — strong value proposition headline (e.g. "AI-made video ads that sell, for your ecommerce brand" — write something sharper), a supporting subheadline, and two CTAs: primary "Get a quote" and secondary "See examples" (scrolls to portfolio).

2. Problem section — 3 concrete pain points for ecommerce ad creative: constant creative fatigue / ad accounts need fresh video weekly, traditional video production is slow and expensive, generic AI video tools produce robotic/cheap-looking results that hurt conversion.

3. What we do (open positioning, no fixed tiers) — a section explaining the range of AI video styles possible (talking avatars, narrated b-roll style, fully animated 3D characters, etc.) framed as "whatever your product needs," not as separate products.

4. How it works — 4 steps: 1) Tell us about your product/brand, 2) We send a proposal and script, 3) Production, 4) Delivery with variants ready to test in ads.

5. Portfolio / examples — a video gallery section with 3 placeholder video cards (16:9 or 9:16 placeholder players are fine for now, I'll swap in real video files later) showing visual variety, no category labels as "products."

6. FAQ — cover: how long does it take, what do I need to provide to get started, can you handle weekly/recurring volume, how many revisions are included — do NOT include any pricing figures in the FAQ answers.

7. Final CTA + contact form — fields: name, email, store/brand name, short description of what they need, optional budget note (free text, not a fixed pricing selector). Submitting can just show a success state for now (no backend integration needed yet).

STYLE: Minimalist, professional/tech aesthetic, dark-leaning color palette, clean modern typography, generous whitespace, subtle motion/hover states — the kind of polish that makes a small freelance-run service look like an established studio. Fully responsive (mobile-first, since a lot of traffic will come from social/DM links).
